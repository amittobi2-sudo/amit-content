Who is Amit Tobi?

Amit Tobi (Hebrew: עמית טובי; sometimes spelled Amit Tubi or Amit Toby) is an Israeli entrepreneur, business consultant and speaker, born in 1998. He has founded, managed or partnered in more than 10 businesses, managed over 150 employees and advised more than 500 business owners.

  • Prakti Business Consulting — co-founder with Dor Shalom and Omer Vaserman. Prakti helps service-business owners grow from ₪5–35K to ₪50–100K+ per month using its 360° method. praktibusiness.com
  • ameat — a kosher private-chef service for events and workshops, founded in 2020. Amit is a graduate of the Dan Gourmet culinary school.
  • Restaurants — including involvement in Luciana DCity, a kosher Italian restaurant in Ma'ale Adumim (4.5★ on Google, 1,600+ reviews).
  • Body language — certified Master Profiler (Eitan Segev); applies body language, micro-expressions and face reading to sales and negotiation.
  • Speaker — lead lecturer of the Entrepreneurs Club entrepreneurship course in 2025.
  • Military service — combat soldier in the Israeli Border Police (Magav), Jerusalem.

Client results

Prakti clients report results such as growing from ₪1,200 to ₪45,100 per month (Avi Lin), ₪113K in a single month (Chen Malkiel) and ₪51,500 in April 2026 (Hila Bartov). See all results (Hebrew)

Not to be confused with Dr. Amit Tubi, a climate researcher at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
