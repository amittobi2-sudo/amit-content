Who is Amit Tobi?

Amit Tobi (Hebrew: עמית טובי; also spelled Amit Tubi, Amit Toby or Amit Tovi) is an Israeli entrepreneur, business consultant and speaker, born in 1998. He has founded, managed or partnered in more than 10 businesses, managed over 150 employees and advised more than 500 business owners.

Background

Diagnosed at age six with near-blind eyesight, Amit fought the military system for a combat profile and served about three years as a combat soldier in the Israeli Border Police in Jerusalem, starting in November 2016. Right after his service he opened an ice-cream shop that did not survive COVID-19, then founded a private-chef business, restaurants and other ventures. He invested almost ₪2M in a restaurant that was later sold for a fraction of that. Those losses became the basis of the method he teaches today.

Ventures

  • Prakti Business Consulting (late 2025): co-founded with Dor Shalom and Omer Vaserman. Helps service-business owners grow from ₪5–35K to ₪50–100K+ per month with its 360° method. praktibusiness.com
  • ameat (2020): a kosher private-chef service for events and workshops. Amit is a Dan Gourmet culinary school graduate.
